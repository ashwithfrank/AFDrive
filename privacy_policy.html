AFDrive is developed by Ashwith Frank / AlphaOmega Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use AFDrive.

Summary

AFDrive does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data with us or with any third party. AFDrive has no servers of ours, no analytics, no advertising, and no user accounts. Everything the app does happens on your own device and, when you choose to connect two devices together, over your own local Wi-Fi network — never over the internet.

What AFDrive does with your data

  • Files you share. When you use Server Mode, files you choose to make available are stored in a folder private to the app on your device. When another device connects in Client Mode and you choose to upload, download, rename, or delete a file, that action happens directly between the two devices over your local network. We never see, store, or have access to any of these files.
  • Server credentials. If you enable "Secure server," the username and a one-way hash of the password you set are stored only on your device, using Android's encrypted storage (Android Keystore). The plain-text password is never stored.
  • Saved logins. If you choose "Remember this login" when connecting to another device's server, that login is stored only on your device, in the same encrypted storage.
  • Settings. Your theme preference, default server name/port, and recently connected servers are stored only on your device.

None of the above is ever sent to us, because AFDrive has no backend server operated by us to send it to.

Permissions AFDrive requests, and why

  • Camera — used only to scan a QR code containing another AFDrive server's local network address, so you can pair two devices. AFDrive does not capture, store, or transmit photos or video.
  • Local network access (Wi-Fi state) — used to display your device's own local IP address when hosting a server, and to communicate with another AFDrive server on the same network.

AFDrive does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, SMS, or any other permission not listed above.

Third parties

AFDrive does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. If this ever changes in a future version, this policy will be updated first, and the change will be reflected in the app's Play Store Data Safety section.

Children's privacy

AFDrive does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all, as described above.

Changes to this policy

If AFDrive's data practices ever change, this policy will be updated, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect that.
